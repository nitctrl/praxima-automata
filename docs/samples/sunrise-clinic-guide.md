# Sunrise Clinic: Patient Guide

This guide is fictional sample content for testing document upload, review, publishing and
search in the Praxima console. It is not medical advice.

## About us

Sunrise Clinic is a family clinic founded in 2012. We offer general consultations, specialist
consultations and routine diagnostic tests under one roof. Our team includes general
physicians, a cardiologist, a paediatrician and trained nursing staff.

We have two branches: Andheri West in Mumbai and Kothrud in Pune. Both branches follow the
same timings, registration process and policies described in this guide.

## Timings

The clinic is open Monday to Saturday from 9 in the morning to 8 in the evening. The clinic
is closed on Sundays and on national holidays.

The pharmacy counter closes at 7:30 in the evening. Diagnostic tests such as ECG and blood
tests are done between 9 in the morning and 5 in the evening.

Individual doctors have their own consultation hours. Please ask the reception for a
specific doctor's schedule.

## Registration

New patients register once at the reception desk before their first consultation.
Registration takes about ten minutes.

Please bring a government photo ID such as an Aadhaar card, PAN card or passport, and your
previous prescriptions and test reports if you have them. For children, a parent or guardian
registers on the child's behalf.

After registration you receive a patient ID card. Please carry it on every visit.

## Appointments

Specialist consultations need an appointment. General consultations are available on a walk-in
basis during clinic hours, subject to waiting time.

You can request an appointment by phone. Our team will call you back to confirm the date and
time. A request is not a confirmed booking until a staff member confirms it.

To cancel or change an appointment, please call at least two hours in advance.

## Payment and insurance

We accept cash, UPI, and debit and credit cards. Payment is made at the reception after the
consultation.

We accept cashless insurance from major insurers for eligible tests and procedures. Please
bring your insurance card and a photo ID. For reimbursement claims, ask the reception for an
itemised bill and a copy of your reports.

## Facilities

Both branches have wheelchair access, a lift, a waiting area with drinking water, and clean
restrooms. The Andheri West branch has two-wheeler parking in the building. The Kothrud
branch has paid car parking next door.

Reports for blood tests are usually ready the same evening and can be collected at the
reception or received by email.

## Visitor policy

One attendant may accompany each patient. Children under twelve visiting as attendants must
be supervised at all times.

Please keep phones on silent in consultation rooms. Food is not allowed in the waiting area.

## Emergencies

Sunrise Clinic is not an emergency hospital. In a medical emergency, call 112 or go to the
nearest hospital emergency department immediately.
